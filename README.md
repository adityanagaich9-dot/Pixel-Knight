# ⚔️ Pixel Knight — a adventure game

Step into the dark fantasy realm of **Pixel Knight**, a fast-paced 2D action platformer filled with challenging dungeons, traps, brutal monsters, and epic boss battles.

Choose your champion, master their unique combat skills, and fight your way through the forgotten lands!

---

### 🌟 Game Highlights:
- 🛡️ **12 Playable Champions**: Play as Frost Knight, Shadow Knight, Inferno Knight, Glacier Knight, Bone Reaper, Thorn Ranger, Sun Paladin, Void Walker, Iron Colossus, Night Assassin, Crimson King, and the endgame Eternal Knight.
- ⚡ **Unique Hero Abilities**: Freeze nearby enemies with *Frost Nova*, summon waves of fire with *Flame Wave*, teleport with *Rift Step*, or open a cosmic vortex with *Celestial Rift*.
- ⚔️ **Fluid Combat System**: Combo strikes, shield guarding, perfect parries, air jumping, and high-speed dodging.
- 👹 **10 Handcrafted Stages & Brutal Bosses**: Face dangerous enemies including skeletons, wisps, turrets, and massive titans like the Frost Guardian and the Necromancer.
- 🪙 **Hero Progression & Upgrades**: Collect gold and ancient boss relics to unlock powerful heroes and runes.
- 📱 **PC & Mobile Support**: Seamless keyboard controls for desktop and on-screen touch controls for mobile.

---

### 🎮 Controls:
- **Move**: `A` / `D` or `Left` / `Right` Arrow keys
- **Jump**: `Space` / `W` / `Up` Arrow (Press twice for Double Jump)
- **Attack / Slash**: `J` or `Left Mouse Button`
- **Special Hero Ability**: `Q`
- **Dash**: `Shift`
- **Shield / Parry**: Hold `E`
- **Roll**: `S` + Movement Direction
